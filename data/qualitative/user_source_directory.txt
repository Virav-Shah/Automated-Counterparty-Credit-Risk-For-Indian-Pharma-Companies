### 5. Standard Page / Section Mapping for Qualitative Evidence Extraction

To extract concentrated currency exposure, geographic breakdown, and qualitative risks, standard headings in the Annual Reports across all 20 companies include:

1. **Geographic Concentration & Exports:** *Management Discussion and Analysis (MD&A) $\rightarrow$ Segment-wise or Geography-wise Performance* (typically Pages 60–90 of the Integrated Report).
2. **Currency Exposure & Financial Risks:** *Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements $\rightarrow$ Note on Financial Instruments & Risk Management (Market Risk / Foreign Currency Sensitivity)* (typically Pages 180–220).
3. **ESG & Operational Risks:** *Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR)* section of the report.
