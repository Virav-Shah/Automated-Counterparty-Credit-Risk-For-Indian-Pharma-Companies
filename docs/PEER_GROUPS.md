# Peer-group rationale

Assignments supplied by the user are based on business model and operating characteristics. They do not use market capitalization or credit-risk scores. The financial credit-risk score is an output compared within groups.

## Global Generics & Diversified — 8 companies

Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy’s, Cipla, Zydus Lifesciences, Lupin, Aurobindo Pharma, Glenmark Pharma and Torrent Pharma.

The comparison focuses on multi-market formulation/generics businesses, international commercial operations, regulatory approvals, product portfolios, currency exposures and recurring investment requirements. Within the group, Sun’s specialty portfolio, Cipla’s respiratory franchises, Torrent’s branded-market orientation and other companies’ generic/API mixes remain issuer-specific differences. Torrent’s membership is the user’s analytical classification; it does not imply the same export mix as Aurobindo.

## India-Focused / Branded Formulations — 5 companies

Mankind Pharma, Alkem Labs, Abbott India, Ipca Labs and Ajanta Pharma.

The comparison focuses on domestic branded-formulation franchises, commercial distribution, therapy portfolios and the collection/inventory patterns associated with those activities. Export intensity varies substantially; Ajanta’s international branded business and Ipca’s exports require issuer-specific discussion. The group represents comparable operating characteristics rather than uniformly domestic revenues.

## API / CDMO / Contract Manufacturing — 3 companies

Divi’s Labs, Laurus Labs and Piramal Pharma.

The comparison focuses on B2B manufacturing and development relationships, customer contracts, capacity investment, utilization, inventory, development cycles and the cash-flow consequences of capex. Sponsor/customer dependence and pricing conditions can influence earnings and working capital. Laurus is compared against Divi’s and Piramal in this group; Gland has been moved to specialty/platforms under the user’s final assignment.

## Complex / Specialty / Healthcare Platforms — 3 companies

Biocon, Gland Pharma and Jubilant Pharmova.

The comparison captures biologics/biosimilars, complex sterile injectables, specialty products or diversified healthcare platforms. Technical/regulatory requirements, integration, specialized capacity and commercialization are relevant review dimensions. These are heterogeneous specialist businesses, so ratio benchmarks support rather than replace individual issuer assessment. Gland’s B2B characteristics are acknowledged even though its complex-injectable business motivates this classification.

## Benchmark practice and limits

Financial benchmark medians and percentiles use other companies in the same group and fiscal year. Groups of three leave only two comparator companies per issuer. Missing ratios are excluded, with valid comparator counts disclosed. Outliers are review prompts, not proof of financial weakness.

The group rationale is a project methodology supplied by the user. Exact company revenue mixes, operational claims and current regulatory status should be supported with dated annual-report sections when writing the final report. No inference of identical credit quality follows from shared membership.
